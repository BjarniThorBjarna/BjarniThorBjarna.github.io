Kalaha

Trainer

    Rules
    • Pick one of your pits. Its stones are sown one by one, counterclockwise, into the following pits and your own store, skipping the opponent's store.
    • If the last stone lands in your store, you take another turn.
    • If the last stone lands in an empty pit on your side and the pit opposite has stones, you capture both into your store.
    • When either side runs out of stones, the game ends and each player keeps the stones left on their own side. Most stones wins.
